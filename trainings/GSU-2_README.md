# GSU-2 Training Materials

## Overview

This folder contains all training materials for **GSU-2: Facility Use Management and Performance Evaluation**, a session designed for the General Services Unit (GSU) at PSHS-MC. This session transforms how GSU manages facility bookings, vehicle requests, and performance evaluations using Google Workspace tools.

## Teaching Methodology

### Flipped Classroom Approach

**Part 1: Pre-Session Video (10-15 min, asynchronous)**
- Participants watch the video 2-3 days before the in-person session
- Covers analogy, keywords, patterns, and examples
- Includes screen recordings of actual Google Workspace tools
- Ends with a quick check question

**Part 2: In-Person Session (60 min, synchronous, hands-on)**
- Welcome and Q&A on video (3 min)
- Shared Google Calendars for Booking (12 min)
- Digital Permit Forms (12 min)
- Performance Evaluation Scoring (12 min)
- Cleaning Checklist (8 min)
- Audience Practice: Guided exercises (8 min)
- Wrap-up & Scoring (5 min)

### Gamification: Inter-Unit Competition

- Each unit earns points based on participant performance
- Scoring criteria: Completion (40 pts), Accuracy (40 pts), Independence (20 pts)
- Visible leaderboard tracks unit progress across all sessions
- Points accumulate across the entire training program

## Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of GSU-2, participants will be able to:

1. Create and manage shared Google Calendars for facility and vehicle booking per FAM 6.2
2. Create digital permit forms using Google Forms (facilities and vehicles)
3. Build performance evaluation spreadsheets with automatic scoring in Google Sheets
4. Digitize the cleaning checklist using Google Forms per FAM 6.3
5. Create security guard evaluation forms per FAM 6.6
6. Use formulas to calculate total scores and averages
7. Organize GSU documents in Google Drive with proper folder structure

## Important Notes

- **This session is for GSU staff** who manage facilities, vehicles, and performance evaluations
- **Compliance with FAM 6.2, 6.3, and 6.6** is emphasized throughout
- **Permits must be submitted 2 days before** the activity/travel per FAM 6.2
- **Performance evaluations are semi-annual** per FAM 6.3 and 6.6
- **Regular backups** are recommended (download as .ods for LibreOffice)
- **The digital system enhances, not replaces** existing processes
