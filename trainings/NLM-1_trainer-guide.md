## Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

1. Understand what NotebookLM is and why it is useful for meeting minutes
2. Record clear meeting audio using a smartphone voice recorder
3. Upload audio recordings to NotebookLM as a source

5. Use targeted prompts to extract action items, decisions, and discussion points
6. Refine NotebookLM output into a formal PSHS meeting minutes document in Google Docs
7. Apply best practices for audio quality and privacy when recording meetings

### 2. Introduction to NotebookLM (5 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Let's start with the basics. What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is Google's free AI-powered research assistant. It's designed to help you understand and summarize information from various sources—text documents, PDFs, websites, and importantly for us, audio files.

Here's why NotebookLM is perfect for meeting minutes:

**First:** It accepts audio files directly. You don't need to transcribe the recording first—just upload the audio file, and NotebookLM processes it.

**Second:** It generates structured summaries automatically. Within seconds, you get an overview of what was discussed, key topics, and even an audio overview—a podcast-style summary.

**Third:** It provides citations. When NotebookLM gives you information, it links back to the specific point in the audio where that information came from. This makes it easy to verify facts and add context.

**Fourth:** It's conversational. You can ask NotebookLM questions about the meeting, and it will answer based on the audio source.

**Fifth:** It respects your privacy. Your data stays in your Google account and is not used to train AI models.

### 3. Recording Meeting Audio on Your Phone (8 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"The first step in creating AI-powered meeting minutes is recording clear audio. The better your audio quality, the better NotebookLM will understand the meeting.

Here are best practices for recording meeting audio on your phone:

**Positioning:**
- Place your phone near the center of the table, not at the edge
- If possible, use a stand or prop to keep it upright and stable
- Avoid covering the microphone with your hand or other objects

**Environment:**
- Choose a quiet room if possible
- Close doors and windows to reduce outside noise
- Turn off fans or air conditioners that create background noise
- Avoid rooms with echoes (rooms with lots of hard surfaces)

**During the Meeting:**
- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- Ask participants to speak one at a time
- If someone is speaking softly, ask them to speak up
- Avoid side conversations that will be picked up by the recorder
- Start the recording before the meeting begins to capture introductions

**Technical Settings:**
- Use the highest quality recording setting available
- Record in a common format like MP3 or WAV
- Save the file with a clear name (e.g., 'Staff_Meeting_2025-04-17.mp3')

**Before Recording:**
- Test your voice recorder app before the meeting
- Ensure you have enough storage space on your phone
- Check that your phone is charged or plugged in
- Inform all participants that the meeting is being recorded

Let me demonstrate how to use a typical voice recorder app."

[DEMONSTRATION: Show a smartphone screen with a voice recorder app. Demonstrate starting a recording, pausing, stopping, and saving.]

**Trainer Script:**

"Most voice recorder apps work similarly:
1. Open the app
2. Tap the record button (usually a red circle)
3. The app starts recording and shows a timer
4. You can pause if needed
5. When done, tap stop
6. Name and save the file

After the meeting, you'll need to transfer the audio file to your computer. You can:
- Email the file to yourself
- Upload to Google Drive and download on your computer
- Use a USB cable to transfer directly
- Use a cloud service like Dropbox or OneDrive

Any questions about recording meeting audio?"

**Anticipated Questions & Answers:**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| "What if I forget to start the recording?" | That happens! For important meetings, consider having a backup recorder or asking a colleague to also record. |
| "Can I use a dedicated audio recorder instead of a phone?" | Absolutely! Dedicated recorders often have better microphones and longer battery life. The process is the same—transfer the file to your computer and upload to NotebookLM. |
| "What if the meeting is very long (over an hour)?" | NotebookLM can handle long audio files, but consider breaking very long meetings into segments for easier processing. |
| "Do I need to edit the audio before uploading?" | No need for extensive editing. However, you might want to trim silence at the beginning or end. Keep the file as-is for the most accurate results. |

### 4. Uploading Audio to NotebookLM (8 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Now let's get our audio into NotebookLM. Here's the step-by-step process:

**Step 1: Open NotebookLM**
- Go to notebooklm.google.com in your web browser
- Sign in with your Google account (the same one you use for Gmail and Google Drive)

**Step 2: Create a New Notebook**
- Click 'Create' or the '+' button
- Give your notebook a clear name (e.g., 'Staff Meeting - April 17, 2025')
- Click 'Create'

**Step 3: Add Your Audio Source**
- In your new notebook, click 'Add Source'
- Select 'Audio' from the options
- Click 'Upload' and select your audio file from your computer
- Wait for the upload to complete—this may take a few minutes depending on file size

**Step 4: Wait for Processing**
- NotebookLM will process the audio file
- You'll see a progress indicator
- Once complete, you'll see the audio source listed in your notebook

Let me demonstrate this process."

### 6. Prompting for Meeting Minutes (10 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Prompting is how you get NotebookLM to give you exactly what you need for meeting minutes. Think of it as asking a very capable assistant to extract specific information from the meeting.

Here are the most useful prompts for meeting minutes:

**Prompt 1: Extract Action Items**
```
List all action items from this meeting, including:
- What needs to be done
- Who is responsible
- Any deadlines mentioned
```

**Prompt 2: Extract Decisions**
```
List all decisions made during this meeting, including:
- What was decided
- The context or background
- Any conditions or next steps
```

**Prompt 3: Summarize by Agenda Item**
```
For each agenda item discussed, provide:
- A brief summary of the discussion
- Key points raised
- Any decisions or action items related to it
```

**Prompt 4: Identify Attendees and Their Contributions**
```
List all participants who spoke during the meeting and summarize their main contributions or points made.
```

**Prompt 5: Extract Follow-Up Requirements**
```
What follow-up actions are needed after this meeting? Include any items that need to be brought to future meetings.
```

Let me demonstrate these prompts."

[DEMONSTRATION: Type each prompt and show the response. Highlight how the responses are structured and include citations.]

### 10. Wrap-up, Privacy, and Best Practices (5 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Before we wrap up, let's cover some important considerations for using NotebookLM responsibly.

**Privacy and Ethical Considerations:**

1. **Authorization:** Only record meetings where you have explicit authorization. Inform all participants that the meeting is being recorded.

2. **Informed Consent:** Clearly state the purpose of recording—for generating meeting minutes—and who will have access to the recording.

3. **Data Handling:**
   - NotebookLM data stays within your Google account
   - Delete audio sources after minutes are finalized
   - Don't share notebooks with unauthorized people

4. **Confidential Meetings:**
   - For HR matters, legal proceedings, or sensitive discussions, consult your unit head first
   - Consider whether AI processing is appropriate for the content
   - Some meetings may require manual transcription only

5. **Verification:**
   - Always review and verify AI-generated content
   - Use citations to check accuracy
   - The AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment

**Best Practices for Success:**

1. **Audio Quality:** The better your audio, the better your results. Follow the recording best practices we covered.

2. **Prompt Library:** Develop and save your favorite prompts for reuse. This saves time and ensures consistency.

3. **Templates:** Use the provided meeting minutes template (NLM-1_template.csv) to maintain consistent formatting.

4. **Practice:** Like any skill, using NotebookLM effectively improves with practice. Start with less critical meetings to build confidence.

5. **Backup Plans:** Always have a backup recording method. Technology can fail, and you don't want to lose important meeting content.

**Limitations of NotebookLM:**

1. **Audio Quality Dependency:** Poor audio quality leads to poor results. Background noise, multiple speakers talking at once, and quiet voices can reduce accuracy.

2. **Context Understanding:** NotebookLM may miss nuances, sarcasm, or implicit understanding that a human would catch.

3. **Technical Limitations:** File size limits, processing time, and occasional errors can occur.

4. **Not a Replacement for Human Judgment:** Always review and edit AI-generated content. The final responsibility for accurate minutes remains with you.

**Where to Find Help:**

- Quick Reference Card: Keep it handy for step-by-step reminders
- NotebookLM Help Center: support.google.com/notebooklm
- ITU: For technical issues with NotebookLM access
- Colleagues: Share tips and prompt examples

**Follow-Up Support:**

After this session, you can:
- Refer to your Quick Reference Card
- Use the provided template for consistent formatting
- Contact ITU for technical support
- Request follow-up sessions for advanced prompting techniques

## Troubleshooting Tips

### Audio Upload Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| File won't upload | Check file format (use MP3 or WAV), check file size limits, try a different browser |
| Upload is very slow | Check internet connection, try a smaller file first, try uploading at a different time |
| Processing seems stuck | Refresh the page, try uploading again, check if the file is corrupted |

### NotebookLM Not Responding

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| NotebookLM won't open | Clear browser cache, try a different browser, check internet connection |
| Prompts not generating responses | Check if audio is fully processed, try a simpler prompt, refresh the page |
| Citations not working | Make sure the audio source is loaded, try clicking on different parts of the response |

### Poor Results

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Summary doesn't match meeting | Check audio quality, try a different prompt, verify the correct file was uploaded |
| Action items missing | Use specific prompts for action items, check if action items were clearly stated in the meeting |
| Decisions unclear | Prompt for decisions with context, use citations to find where decisions were discussed |

### Technical Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't access NotebookLM | Verify Google account is working, check if NotebookLM is available in your region, contact ITU |
| Audio file won't play | Check file format, try a different audio player, ensure file isn't corrupted |
| Can't copy content to Google Docs | Try selecting text differently, use keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V), check browser permissions |
