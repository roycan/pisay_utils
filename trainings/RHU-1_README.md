# RHU-1 Training Materials

## Overview

This folder contains all training materials for **RHU-1: Residence Hall Administration and Monitoring**, a session designed for the Residence Hall Unit (RHU) at PSHS-MC. This session transforms how RHU manages residence hall operations using Google Workspace tools.

## Teaching Methodology

### Flipped Classroom Approach

**Part 1: Pre-Session Video (10-15 min, asynchronous)**
- Participants watch the video 2-3 days before the in-person session
- Covers analogy, keywords, patterns, and examples
- Includes screen recordings of actual Google Workspace tools
- Ends with a quick check question

**Part 2: In-Person Session (75 min, synchronous, hands-on)**
- Welcome and Q&A on video (5 min)
- Digital Residence Hall Application (15 min)
- Resident Management Tracker (15 min)
- Contract Templates & Waivers (10 min)
- Good Housekeeping Checklist (10 min)
- Leave Pass Tracking (10 min)
- Audience Practice: Guided exercises (7 min)
- Wrap-up & Scoring (3 min)

### Gamification: Inter-Unit Competition

- Each unit earns points based on participant performance
- Scoring criteria: Completion (40 pts), Accuracy (40 pts), Independence (20 pts)
- Visible leaderboard tracks unit progress across all sessions
- Points accumulate across the entire training program

## Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of RHU-1, participants will be able to:

1. Create a digital Residence Hall Application Form using Google Forms
2. Build a resident management tracker in Google Sheets with room assignments and contract status
3. Create and use Google Docs templates for Residence Hall Contracts and Parent/Guardian Waivers
4. Digitize the Good Housekeeping Checklist using Google Forms
5. Track Leave Pass and Return Slips with automatic timestamps in Google Sheets
6. Organize RHU documents in Google Drive following proper folder structure
7. Apply LibreOffice as offline backup for RHU documents (optional)

## Important Notes

- **This session is for RHU staff** who manage residence hall operations
- **Compliance with SSM 5.1 and 5.2** is emphasized throughout
- **Physical signatures are still required** for contracts (until digital signatures are adopted)
- **Parents can still submit paper forms** — RHU staff can enter data into the digital system
- **The digital system enhances, not replaces** existing processes
- **Regular backups** are recommended (download as .ods for LibreOffice)
