# Session-0 Hands-On Exercise: Google Workspace Fundamentals

**Duration:** 20 minutes
**Format:** Guided step-by-step exercise
**Prerequisites:** Watched pre-session video

---

## Exercise Scenario

You are a PSHS-MC staff member preparing for the Google Workspace training. Your task is to practice using Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, and Google Chat to complete a series of tasks that simulate real work scenarios.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Gmail Essentials (8 minutes)

#### Task 1: Compose an Email with CC/BCC (3 minutes)

1. Open your web browser and go to `mail.google.com`
2. Click the **Compose** button (pencil icon) in the top-left corner
3. In the **To** field, type your own PSHS email address (for practice)
4. Click **Cc** and type a colleague's email address
5. In the **Subject** field, type: `Practice Email - Session 0 Training`
6. In the email body, type:
   ```
   Dear Colleague,

   This is a practice email for the Google Workspace Fundamentals training.

   Best regards,
   [Your Name]
   ```
7. Click the **Attach files** icon (paperclip) and select a small file from your computer
8. Click **Send**

**Expected Outcome:** The email is sent to yourself and CC'd to your colleague. You should see a "Message sent" confirmation.

[SCREENSHOT: Compose window with To, Cc, Subject, and Attachment fields filled]

---

#### Task 2: Create and Apply Labels (3 minutes)

1. On the left sidebar of Gmail, scroll down to **Labels**
2. Click **+ Create** next to "Labels"
3. In the dialog box, type: `Training`
4. Click **Create**
5. Find the email you just sent (check your **Sent** folder or **Inbox**)
6. Select the email by clicking the checkbox next to it
7. Click the **Label** icon (tag icon) in the toolbar
8. Check the box next to **Training**
9. Click **Apply**
10. Create another label called `Urgent`
11. Apply the **Urgent** label to the same email

**Expected Outcome:** The email now has both "Training" and "Urgent" labels. You should see colored labels next to the email subject.

[SCREENSHOT: Email with "Training" and "Urgent" labels applied]

---

#### Task 3: Search for Emails (2 minutes)

1. Click in the search bar at the top of Gmail
2. Type: `Session 0`
3. Press **Enter**
4. Observe the search results
5. Click the search icon (magnifying glass) in the search bar
6. In the advanced search dialog, try filtering by:
   - **From:** Your own email address
   - **Has attachment:** Check the box
7. Click **Search**

**Expected Outcome:** Gmail shows all emails matching your search criteria. Advanced search filters help you find specific emails.

[SCREENSHOT: Search bar and advanced search dialog]

---

### Part 2: Google Drive (6 minutes)

#### Task 4: Create Folder Structure (3 minutes)

1. Open a new tab and go to `drive.google.com`
2. Click **+ New** → **Folder**
3. Name the folder: `Session0_Practice_YourName` (replace "YourName" with your actual name)
4. Double-click to open the folder
5. Click **+ New** → **Folder**
6. Name it: `01_Emails`
7. Create two more folders inside:
   - `02_Documents`
   - `03_Calendar`
8. Click the back arrow to return to your main Drive view

**Expected Outcome:** You have a main folder named "Session0_Practice_YourName" with three numbered subfolders inside.

[SCREENSHOT: Folder structure in Google Drive]

---

#### Task 5: Upload and Share a File (3 minutes)

1. Open the `02_Documents` folder you just created
2. Click **+ New** → **File upload**
3. Select a document or image from your computer
4. Wait for the upload to complete
5. Right-click the uploaded file
6. Click **Share**
7. In the "Add people and groups" field, type a colleague's email address
8. Under "General access," click **Change**
9. Select **Anyone with the link** → **Editor**
10. Click **Copy link**
11. Click **Done**
12. Paste the link in a new email to yourself (for reference)

**Expected Outcome:** The file is uploaded to Google Drive and shared with your colleague. They should receive an email notification with access to the file.

[SCREENSHOT: Share dialog with email address and permission settings]

---

### Part 3: Google Calendar (3 minutes)

#### Task 6: Create a Calendar Event (3 minutes)

1. Open a new tab and go to `calendar.google.com`
2. Click on tomorrow's date at 10:00 AM
3. In the event popup, enter:
   - **Add title:** `Unit Meeting`
   - **Add description:** `Regular unit meeting to discuss upcoming projects`
4. Click **Add guests** and type a colleague's email address
5. Click **Add notification**
6. Select **10 minutes before**
7. Click **Save**
8. If prompted, choose to send invitations to guests

**Expected Outcome:** A calendar event is created for tomorrow at 10:00 AM. Your colleague receives an invitation.

[SCREENSHOT: Calendar event creation popup]

---

### Part 4: Google Chat (3 minutes)

#### Task 7: Send a Chat Message (3 minutes)

1. Click the **Chat** icon in Google Workspace (or go to `chat.google.com`)
2. In the left sidebar, find a colleague's name
3. Click on their name to open a chat window
4. Type the following message:
   ```
   Hi! I'm practicing Google Chat for the Session 0 training. How are you doing?
   ```
5. Press **Enter** to send
6. Wait for their response

**Expected Outcome:** Your message is sent to your colleague via Google Chat. You can see the conversation in the chat window.

[SCREENSHOT: Google Chat window with message sent]

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, check these common problems:

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't find sent email | Check the "Sent" folder in Gmail. Verify the email was actually sent. |
| Label not appearing | Make sure you clicked "Apply" after selecting the label. Refresh the page. |
| Can't create folder | Check your internet connection. Try refreshing the page. |
| File upload failed | Check file size (limit is 5TB per file). Verify you have storage space. |
| Can't share file | Verify the recipient has a PSHS Google account. Check the email address for typos. |
| Calendar event not showing | Check that you selected the correct date and time. Refresh the calendar view. |
| Can't send chat message | Verify the recipient is online and has Google Chat enabled. Check your internet connection. |
