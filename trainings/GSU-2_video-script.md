**Target Audience:** GSU staff, Property Custodian, Security Head, Janitorial Services Head

## Keywords and Concepts (2 minutes)

[SCREEN: Bulleted list of keywords appears one by one as narrator speaks]

**Narrator:**

"Before we dive in, let's cover some key terms you'll need to know.

**First: Shared Calendars.** A shared calendar is a Google Calendar that multiple people can access and edit. You can create separate calendars for facilities, vehicles, and equipment. Staff can view availability and book resources without calling GSU.

**Second: Event Booking.** In Google Calendar, an event represents a booking. You can add details like the requestor's name, purpose, equipment needed, and approval status. Events can be color-coded for easy identification.

**Third: Digital Forms.** Instead of paper permit forms, we'll use Google Forms. Staff submit requests online, and all responses are automatically collected in a spreadsheet. This makes processing and tracking much easier.

**Fourth: Automatic Scoring.** In Google Sheets, formulas can calculate total scores automatically. For performance evaluations, you enter scores for each criterion, and the spreadsheet calculates the total and assigns a rating.

**Fifth: Conditional Formatting.** This automatically changes cell colors based on rules. We'll use it to highlight ratings—green for outstanding, yellow for satisfactory, red for poor—so results are instantly visible."

[SCREEN: Visual examples of each concept appear as they're mentioned]

## Pattern Demonstration (8-10 minutes)

### Shared Google Calendars (3 minutes)

[SCREEN: Screen recording of creating a Google Calendar]

**Narrator:**

"Let's start with shared calendars. Go to calendar.google.com. On the left, click the plus sign next to 'Other calendars' and choose 'Create new calendar.'

Name it 'Facility Booking - PSHS-MC' and add a description. Set the time zone to Asia/Manila and click Create.

Now let's share it. Hover over the calendar name, click the three dots, and choose 'Settings and sharing.' Under 'Share with specific people,' enter email addresses of authorized staff. Set permission level to 'Make changes and manage sharing' for GSU staff, and 'See all event details' for requestors.

Create a second calendar for vehicle booking using the same process.

[SCREEN: Creating a calendar event]

**Narrator:**

"To book a facility, click on the date and time. Add an event title like 'Science Lab 1 - Grade 7 Class.' In the description, add details: requestor name, purpose, number of participants, and equipment needed. Click Save. The event appears on the shared calendar for everyone to see."

### Digital Permit Forms (3 minutes)

[SCREEN: Screen recording of creating a Google Form]

**Narrator:**

"Now let's create digital permit forms. Go to forms.google.com and click Blank. Name it 'Permit to Use School Facilities.'

Add sections for requestor information: Name, Department, Email, Contact Number.

Add facility details: Facility to be used, date, time, purpose.

Add equipment needed: Checkboxes for projector, microphone, tables, chairs, and other equipment.

Add approval sections: Division Chief recommendation, FAD Chief approval, and final status.

Click Settings and enable 'Collect email addresses.' Click Responses and link to a Google Sheet. This will collect all permit requests in one place.

Create a similar form for vehicle permits with vehicle-specific details."

### Performance Evaluation Scoring (2 minutes)

[SCREEN: Screen recording of creating a Google Sheet]

**Narrator:**

"Now let's create a performance evaluation spreadsheet with automatic scoring. Go to drive.google.com, click New, and select Google Sheets. Name it 'Janitorial_Performance_Evaluation_2026.'

Set up column headers: Employee Name, Date, Evaluator, and evaluation criteria like Attendance, Punctuality, Quality of Work, Completeness of Tasks, and so on.

Add a Total Score column. In the first cell, enter the SUM formula to add up all the scores.

Add a Rating column. Enter an IF formula that assigns a rating based on the total score: 90+ is Outstanding, 80+ is Very Satisfactory, and so on.

Add conditional formatting to highlight ratings in different colors. Now when you enter scores, the total and rating calculate automatically."

### Cleaning Checklist (2 minutes)

[SCREEN: Screen recording of creating a checklist form]

**Narrator:**

"Finally, let's create a digital cleaning checklist. Go to forms.google.com and create a new form named 'Cleaning_Checklist.'

Add area information: Area being cleaned, date, janitor name.

Add cleaning tasks: Floor swept and mopped, surfaces dusted, trash bins emptied, restrooms sanitized, windows cleaned, mirrors polished. Use checkboxes with options 'Completed' and 'Not Completed.'

Add supplies used: Checkboxes for broom, mop, disinfectant, glass cleaner, trash bags.

Add overall assessment: Overall cleanliness rating and comments.

Link the form to a Google Sheet to collect all inspection data. This makes it easy to track cleaning quality and identify areas needing improvement."

## Production Notes for Video Recording

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum
- **Audio:** Clear narration, no background noise
- **Screen Recording:** Use zoom-in features for small text and buttons
- **Pacing:** Speak clearly and at a moderate pace; allow pauses for viewers to process information

### Visual Style
- **Color Scheme:** Use PSHS branding colors where appropriate
- **Font:** Clean, readable sans-serif (Arial, Roboto, or similar)
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts or fades; avoid flashy transitions
- **Annotations:** Use arrows, circles, or highlights to draw attention to specific elements

### Accessibility
- **Captions:** Include closed captions for accessibility
- **Visual Cues:** Use visual indicators to reinforce concepts
- **Pacing:** Allow time for viewers to read on-screen text before moving on

### Distribution
- **Format:** MP4 or WebM
- **File Size:** Compress to under 500MB for easy sharing via email or Google Drive
- **Hosting:** Upload to Google Drive and share link 2-3 days before the in-person session
- **Deadline:** Ensure video is available to participants at least 48 hours before the session

---

## Important Notes for the Trainer

1. **Emphasize FAM Compliance:** Throughout the video, reference FAM 6.2, 6.3, and 6.6 to show how digital tools comply with manual requirements.

2. **Use GSU-Specific Examples:** When demonstrating, use actual GSU scenarios (e.g., booking the auditorium, evaluating janitorial staff).

3. **Highlight Efficiency Gains:** Emphasize time savings—no more manual scheduling, instant availability checks, automatic score calculations.

4. **Focus on Practical Tools:** Concentrate on tools GSU will use daily—calendars, permits, evaluations, checklists.

5. **Mention Paper Backup:** Remind participants that paper forms can still be used and data entered into the digital system.
