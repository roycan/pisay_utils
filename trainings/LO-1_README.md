# LO-1 Training Materials

## Overview

This folder contains all training materials for **LO-1: LibreOffice as Offline Backup**, a supplemental session recommended for all PSHS-MC administrative units. This session ensures business continuity by providing staff with the skills to work offline when internet is unavailable or Google Workspace is temporarily down.

## Why LibreOffice?

LibreOffice is essential for PSHS-MC for several reasons:

1. **Offline Backup:** When internet is down, staff can continue working
2. **Cost-Effective:** Completely free and open-source—no licensing costs
3. **Compatibility:** Works with Microsoft Office (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx) and Google Workspace formats
4. **Campus-Wide Deployment:** Will be installed on all PSHS computers before full training rollout
5. **Business Continuity:** Ensures operations continue during internet outages

## File Formats

| Application | LibreOffice Format | Microsoft Format | Google Format |
|-------------|-------------------|------------------|---------------|
| Documents | .odt (OpenDocument Text) | .docx (Word 2007+) | Google Docs |
| Spreadsheets | .ods (OpenDocument Spreadsheet) | .xlsx (Excel 2007+) | Google Sheets |
| Presentations | .odp (OpenDocument Presentation) | .pptx (PowerPoint 2007+) | Google Slides |

**Note:** LibreOffice uses open formats (.odt, .ods, .odp) by default. These are standardized formats that any software can open.

## Teaching Methodology

### Flipped Classroom Approach

**Part 1: Pre-Session Video (10-15 min, asynchronous)**
- Participants watch the video 2-3 days before the in-person session
- Covers analogy, keywords, patterns, and examples
- Includes screen recordings of LibreOffice applications
- Ends with a quick check question

**Part 2: In-Person Session (60 min, synchronous, hands-on)**
- Welcome and Q&A on video (5 min)
- Introduction to LibreOffice (5 min)
- LibreOffice Writer (12 min)
- LibreOffice Calc (12 min)
- LibreOffice Impress (8 min)
- Offline Workflow (8 min)
- Audience Practice: Guided exercises (7 min)
- Contest Activity: Independent challenge (3 min)
- Wrap-up & Scoring (5 min)

### Gamification: Inter-Unit Competition

- Each unit earns points based on participant performance
- Scoring criteria: Completion (40 pts), Accuracy (40 pts), Independence (20 pts)
- Visible leaderboard tracks unit progress across all sessions
- Points accumulate across the entire training program

## Google Drive Desktop App (Optional)

For enhanced offline access, participants can install the Google Drive desktop app:

1. Go to `drive.google.com`
2. Click **Settings** gear → **Get Drive for desktop**
3. Download and install the app
4. Sign in with PSHS Google account
5. A Google Drive folder appears on the computer
6. Files sync automatically when online

This is optional but recommended for frequent offline work.

## Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of LO-1, participants will be able to:

1. Understand what LibreOffice is and why it's needed as an offline backup
2. Use LibreOffice Writer to create, open, and save documents (.odt, .docx)
3. Use LibreOffice Calc to create, open, and save spreadsheets (.ods, .xlsx)
4. Use LibreOffice Impress to create, open, and save presentations (.odp, .pptx)
5. Download Google Workspace files in LibreOffice formats for offline use
6. Work offline in LibreOffice and upload files back to Google Drive when online
7. Apply basic keyboard shortcuts and tips for efficiency
8. Understand the complete offline workflow
