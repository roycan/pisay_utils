## Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

1. Navigate the Google Workspace ecosystem and understand the purpose of each tool
2. Comply with PSHS email policies per FAM 12.2 Section 3.2 (official use only, no personal/commercial accounts, prohibited services)
3. Use Gmail essentials: composing, replying, CC/BCC, attachments, labels, search
4. Organize files in Google Drive with proper folder structure and sharing permissions
5. Create and manage Google Calendar events with attendees and reminders
6. Distinguish when to use Google Chat vs Gmail for communication
7. Understand LibreOffice as an offline backup alternative (once deployed campus-wide)
8. Apply basic keyboard shortcuts and tips for efficiency

### 2. Gmail Essentials & PSHS Email Policies (15 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Let's start with Gmail, which is your primary communication tool at PSHS. But before we dive into the features, we need to understand the policies that govern its use.

Per FAM 12.2 Section 3.2, here are the key policies you MUST know:

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.1:** Email accounts are granted to regular employees and students only. You are responsible for maintaining your email files.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.2:** Only the ISA (Information Systems Administrator) has authority to create and issue official email accounts.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.3:** School email shall be used for ACADEMIC and PSHS-RELATED communications ONLY.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.4 (CRITICAL):** The use of school email for personal, commercial, or religious purposes such as Netflix, Shopee, Lazada, GCash, Spotify, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, Public Fora, and others is STRICTLY PROHIBITED. You cannot use school email to create accounts or download applications from these websites.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.5:** Only official PSHS email accounts can be used to log in to the PSHS Learning Management System.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.6:** PSHS personnel shall use PSHS email for official communication. Using it for personal matters unrelated to school activities is strictly prohibited.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.7:** School email should not be used to forward spam, advertisements, fake news, or other content unrelated to school activities.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.8:** Email privileges are suspended/terminated upon separation, and the email files are surrendered.

**FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.9:** When transferring between PSHS campuses, inform the ISA to coordinate email migration.

Now let's look at Gmail essentials."

#### Step-by-Step Demo Script

**Step 1: Access Gmail**

1. Go to `mail.google.com` or click the Gmail icon in Google Workspace
2. You should be automatically signed in with your PSHS email
3. If not, sign in with your official PSHS email address

**Step 2: Compose an Email**

1. Click the **Compose** button (pencil icon)
2. In the **To** field, type the recipient's email address
3. **CC (Carbon Copy):** Use this to include others who need to see the email but are not the main recipient
4. **BCC (Blind Carbon Copy):** Use this to include recipients without others seeing their email addresses
5. **Subject:** Type a clear, descriptive subject line
6. **Body:** Type your message
7. **Attachments:** Click the paperclip icon to attach files
8. Click **Send**

**Step 3: Use Labels for Organization**

[SCREEN: Showing labels in Gmail]

**Trainer Script:**

"Labels in Gmail work like folders, but better. An email can have multiple labels, making it easier to organize and find.

1. On the left sidebar, scroll down to **Labels**
2. Click **+ Create** to create a new label
3. Name your label (e.g., 'Urgent', 'Project A', 'Training')
4. To label an email: select the email, click the **Label** icon (tag), and choose the label
5. To view labeled emails: click the label name on the left sidebar"

**Step 4: Search in Gmail**

1. Click the search bar at the top
2. Type keywords (e.g., sender name, subject, date)
3. Press Enter
4. Gmail will show all matching emails
5. Click the search icon to use advanced search filters (date range, attachments, etc.)

**Trainer Script:**

"Remember, per FAM 12.2 Section 3.2.7, do NOT use your PSHS email to forward spam, advertisements, or fake news. Your PSHS email is for official business only."

### 3. Google Drive: Files, Folders, Sharing (20 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Now let's move to Google Drive. Think of Google Drive as your digital filing cabinet. Just like a physical filing cabinet, you organize files in folders. But unlike a physical cabinet, you can share files instantly, access them from anywhere, and multiple people can work on them simultaneously."

#### Step-by-Step Demo Script

**Step 1: Access Google Drive**

1. Go to `drive.google.com` or click the Drive icon in Google Workspace
2. You should see your My Drive (your personal storage)

**Step 2: Create a Folder Structure**

[SCREEN: Creating folders in Google Drive]

**Trainer Script:**

"Let's create a folder structure similar to what you'd have in a physical office. For this training, we'll create a basic structure:

1. Click **+ New** → **Folder**
2. Name it: `2026_Work_Files`
3. Double-click to open the folder
4. Create subfolders inside:
   - `01_Official_Documents`
   - `02_Projects`
   - `03_Training_Materials`
   - `04_Templates`

This numbering system helps keep folders in order, just like a physical filing cabinet."

**Step 3: Upload Files**

1. Click **+ New** → **File upload**
2. Select a file from your computer
3. The file will upload to your current folder
4. Alternatively, drag and drop files directly into Google Drive

**Step 4: Share Files and Folders**

[SCREEN: Sharing settings in Google Drive]

**Trainer Script:**

"Sharing is one of Google Drive's most powerful features. Here's how to share:

1. Right-click on a file or folder
2. Click **Share**
3. Enter the email addresses of people you want to share with
4. Choose the access level:
   - **Viewer:** Can only view, not edit
   - **Commenter:** Can view and add comments
   - **Editor:** Can make changes
5. Click **Send**

For folders, you can also generate a shareable link:
1. Click **Share**
2. Click **Change to anyone with the link**
3. Choose the access level
4. Copy the link and share it"

**Step 5: Organize with Stars and Colors**

1. Right-click a file or folder
2. Click **Add star** to mark important items (starred items appear in the Starred section on the left)
3. Click **Change color** to color-code folders (e.g., red for urgent, blue for projects)

**Trainer Script:**

"Remember, per FAM 12.2 Section 3.1.6, PSHS-issued laptops and gadgets should be used solely for academic/official purposes. Store only work-related files in your Google Drive."

### 5. Google Chat vs Gmail (5 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Now let's clarify when to use Google Chat versus Gmail. Both are communication tools, but they serve different purposes."

**Google Chat:**
- **Best for:** Quick, real-time conversations with colleagues
- **Use when:** You need an immediate response, similar to texting or instant messaging
- **Features:** Direct messages, group chats, spaces for team discussions
- **Access:** Click the Chat icon in Google Workspace

**Gmail:**
- **Best for:** Formal, documented communication
- **Use when:** You need a record of the conversation, when sending attachments, or when the recipient may not be available immediately
- **Features:** Email threads, attachments, labels, search
- **Access:** Go to `mail.google.com`

**Trainer Script:**

"Think of it this way: Google Chat is like walking over to a colleague's desk for a quick question. Gmail is like sending a formal memo or letter. Use Chat for quick, informal discussions. Use Gmail for official, documented communication."

### 6. LibreOffice Overview (5 minutes)

**Trainer Script:**

"Finally, let's talk about LibreOffice. LibreOffice is a free, open-source office suite that will be deployed campus-wide. It serves as an offline backup when you don't have internet access."

**What is LibreOffice?**
- **LibreOffice Writer:** Similar to Microsoft Word or Google Docs
- **LibreOffice Calc:** Similar to Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
- **LibreOffice Impress:** Similar to Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides

**When to use LibreOffice:**
- When internet is down and you need to work offline
- When you need to open files in formats that Google doesn't support
- As a backup when Google Workspace is unavailable

**How it works with Google Workspace:**
1. Download Google files as LibreOffice formats (.odt, .ods, .odp)
2. Open and edit in LibreOffice offline
3. When internet returns, upload back to Google Drive
4. Google will convert them back to Google Workspace format

**Trainer Script:**

"LibreOffice will be installed on all PSHS computers before the full training rollout. For now, just know it exists as your offline backup. We'll cover LibreOffice in more detail in session LO-1."

## Troubleshooting Tips

### Common Mistakes and Solutions

| Mistake | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Can't find sent email** | Check the "Sent" folder in Gmail. If not there, verify the email was actually sent (look for "Message sent" confirmation). |
| **Label not appearing** | Make sure you clicked "Apply" after selecting the label. Labels appear next to the email subject. |
| **Can't share folder** | Check that you have the correct email address. Verify the recipient has a PSHS Google account. |
| **Calendar event not showing** | Check that you selected the correct date and time. Refresh the calendar view. |
| **Chat message not sending** | Verify the recipient is online and has Google Chat enabled. Check your internet connection. |
| **File upload failed** | Check file size (Google Drive has a 5TB limit per file). Verify you have internet connection. |
| **Can't find shared folder** | Check your "Shared with me" section in Google Drive. Ask the sharer to verify they used the correct email address. |

### Tips for Success

1. **Use keyboard shortcuts** — Ctrl+C/V to copy/paste, Ctrl+Z to undo
2. **Use descriptive names** — For folders, files, and labels
3. **Check sharing permissions** — Always verify who has access before sharing
4. **Use stars for important items** — Starred items are easy to find
5. **Set calendar reminders** — Never miss an important meeting
6. **Use search effectively** — Gmail and Drive search are powerful tools
7. **Organize as you go** — Don't let files pile up—organize them immediately

## Trainer Notes

- **Pace:** Adjust timing based on participant comfort level. If participants are struggling with a particular tool, spend more time on it and reduce time on others.
- **Analogies:** Emphasize the physical office tools → digital tools analogy throughout. This helps participants relate the new tools to their existing knowledge.

- **Real Examples:** Use actual PSHS scenarios and examples to make the training more relevant.
- **Gamification:** Keep the leaderboard visible and update it after each session. This motivates participants and creates healthy competition.
- **Follow-up:** Schedule a check-in with unit heads 2 weeks after training to address any issues and ensure adoption.
