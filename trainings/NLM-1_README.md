## Why NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is a powerful tool for PSHS-MC staff, especially office secretaries, for several reasons:

1. **Dramatic Time Savings:** What once took hours of manual transcription and writing can now be done in minutes. NotebookLM processes audio and generates structured summaries automatically.

2. **Free and Accessible:** NotebookLM is completely free and part of the Google ecosystem. It works with your existing Google account—no new subscriptions or licenses needed.

3. **Audio-Native Design:** Unlike other AI tools that require transcription first, NotebookLM accepts audio files directly and processes them with context awareness.

4. **Citable Responses:** NotebookLM provides citations that link back to specific points in the audio source, making it easy to verify information and add context to minutes.

5. **Privacy-Respecting:** NotebookLM does not use your data to train AI models. Your meeting recordings stay within your Google account and are not shared externally.

6. **No Complex Prompt Engineering:** For basic meeting minutes, NotebookLM's built-in features (Summary, Key Topics, Audio Overview) work out of the box. Simple prompts can extract action items and decisions without technical expertise.

7. **Aligned with PSHS Google Workspace:** Since PSHS already uses Google Workspace, NotebookLM integrates seamlessly with existing tools like Google Docs and Google Drive.

## Privacy and Ethical Considerations

This training emphasizes responsible use of AI for meeting documentation:

1. **Authorization:** Only record meetings where you have explicit authorization to do so.
2. **Informed Consent:** Inform all participants that the meeting is being recorded for the purpose of generating minutes.
3. **Data Handling:** NotebookLM data stays within your Google account. Delete audio sources after minutes are finalized.
4. **Confidential Meetings:** Do not upload recordings of confidential HR matters, legal proceedings, or sensitive discussions without proper clearance.
5. **Verification:** Always review and verify AI-generated content before issuing official minutes. The AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment.
