# NLM-1 Hands-On Exercise: AI-Powered Meeting Minutes with NotebookLM

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Format:** Guided step-by-step exercise
**Prerequisites:** Watched pre-session video, Session 0 completed

---

## Exercise Scenario

You are a PSHS-MC office secretary who just recorded a staff meeting on your phone. The meeting covered several important topics, including budget planning, new policies, and upcoming events. Your task is to use NotebookLM to extract the key information and create a draft of the meeting minutes.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Upload the Sample Audio to NotebookLM (1 minute)

#### Task 1: Create a New Notebook and Upload Audio

1. Open your web browser and go to **notebooklm.google.com**
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **Create** to start a new notebook
4. Name the notebook: `Practice Meeting - [Your Name]`
5. Click **Create**
6. In your new notebook, click **Add Source**
7. Select **Audio** from the options
8. Click **Upload** and select the sample audio file provided for this exercise
9. Wait for the upload and processing to complete

**Expected Outcome:** You have a new NotebookLM notebook with the sample audio file uploaded and processed. You should see a Summary and Key Topics automatically generated.

[SCREENSHOT: NotebookLM interface with audio source loaded]

---

### Part 2: Generate a Summary and Review Key Topics (1 minute)

#### Task 2: Review the Automatically Generated Content

1. Look at the **Summary** section in your notebook
2. Read through the summary to understand what the meeting was about
3. Click on **Key Topics** to see the main themes discussed
4. Click on one or two key topics to see relevant excerpts from the audio

**Expected Outcome:** You have a good understanding of the meeting's content and main topics.

**Self-Check:**
- [ ] I can see the Summary of the meeting
- [ ] I can see the Key Topics
- [ ] I clicked on a key topic and saw relevant audio excerpts

---

### Part 3: Use Prompts to Extract Action Items and Decisions (2 minutes)

#### Task 3: Extract Action Items

1. In the prompt box at the bottom of the screen, type:
   ```
   List all action items from this meeting, including what needs to be done, who is responsible, and any deadlines mentioned.
   ```
2. Click the send button or press Enter
3. Review the response
4. Notice the citation numbers in brackets—click on one to see where in the audio this information came from

**Expected Outcome:** You have a list of action items with responsible persons and deadlines.

#### Task 4: Extract Decisions

1. In the prompt box, type:
   ```
   List all decisions made during this meeting, including what was decided and the context.
   ```
2. Click the send button or press Enter
3. Review the response
4. Note any citations that help verify the decisions

**Expected Outcome:** You have a list of decisions made during the meeting.

**Self-Check:**
- [ ] I extracted at least 2 action items
- [ ] I identified who is responsible for each action item
- [ ] I found at least 1 decision made during the meeting
- [ ] I clicked on a citation to verify information

---

### Part 4: Create Formal Meeting Minutes in Google Docs (1 minute)

#### Task 5: Draft Meeting Minutes

1. Open a new tab and go to **docs.google.com**
2. Click **Blank** to create a new document
3. Name the document: `Practice Meeting Minutes - [Your Name]`
4. Add a header with the following information:
   - **Title:** Staff Meeting Minutes
   - **Date:** [Use today's date]
   - **Time:** 9:00 AM - 10:30 AM
   - **Location:** Conference Room A
   - **Chairperson:** Dr. Maria Santos
5. Go back to your NotebookLM notebook
6. Copy the **Summary** and paste it into your Google Doc as the introduction
7. Copy the **Action Items** and paste them into your Google Doc
8. Copy the **Decisions** and paste them into your Google Doc
9. Add a brief closing statement: "The meeting adjourned at 10:30 AM."

**Expected Outcome:** You have a draft meeting minutes document with summary, action items, and decisions.

**Self-Check:**
- [ ] I created a new Google Doc
- [ ] I added a meeting header with title, date, time, location, and chairperson
- [ ] I copied the Summary from NotebookLM to the document
- [ ] I copied Action Items from NotebookLM to the document
- [ ] I copied Decisions from NotebookLM to the document
- [ ] I added a closing statement

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Audio file won't upload | Check file format (use MP3 or WAV), check file size, try a different browser |
| NotebookLM is slow to process | Wait a bit longer—processing time depends on file size and length |
| Prompts don't give useful results | Try rephrasing your prompt, be more specific, or break into simpler prompts |
| Can't find action items | Try the prompt: "What tasks need to be completed after this meeting?" |
| Can't find decisions | Try the prompt: "What agreements or conclusions were reached?" |
| Citations don't work | Make sure the audio source is fully loaded, refresh the page if needed |
| Can't copy content to Google Docs | Try selecting text differently, use keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) |
| Google Docs won't open | Check internet connection, try a different browser, ensure you're signed in to Google |

## Audio Recording Best Practices Reminder

When recording actual meetings:

1. **Positioning:** Place your phone near the center of the table
2. **Environment:** Choose a quiet room, close doors and windows
3. **During Meeting:** Speak clearly, ask participants to speak one at a time
4. **Technical:** Use highest quality setting, record in MP3 or WAV format
5. **Before Recording:** Test your app, ensure storage space, inform participants

---

## Privacy Checklist

Before recording and uploading meeting audio:

- [ ] I have authorization to record this meeting
- [ ] All participants have been informed that the meeting is being recorded
- [ ] Participants understand the purpose (generating meeting minutes)
- [ ] I have considered whether this meeting contains confidential information
- [ ] I know who will have access to the recording and minutes
- [ ] I understand that I should delete the audio after minutes are finalized

## Tips for Success

1. **Practice with Non-Critical Meetings:** Start with less important meetings to build confidence
2. **Save Your Prompts:** Keep a document with your favorite prompts for reuse
3. **Use Citations:** Always verify information using the citation links
4. **Iterate:** If the first prompt doesn't give you what you need, try a different approach
5. **Format Consistently:** Use the provided template (NLM-1_template.csv) for consistent formatting
6. **Review Carefully:** Always review and edit AI-generated content before issuing
